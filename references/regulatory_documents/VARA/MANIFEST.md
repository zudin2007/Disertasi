## Model Characteristics: Regional Authority

**Standalone Virtual Asset Regulator:**
- Independent VARA authority (not within central bank)
- Comprehensive VASP regulation in single framework
- Full custody and market conduct authority

**Regional Coordination Role:**
- GCC coordination mechanisms (PS-VARA-02)
- Shared Shariah board coordination
- Regional harmonization leadership

**Islamic Finance Integration:**
- VARA Shariah Board (SC-VARA-01, 02)
- Islamic stablecoin recognition (SC-VARA-02)
- Institutional governance for Islamic VASPs

**Innovation Characteristics:**
- New regulatory authority (established 2022)
- Comprehensive framework (RF-VARA-01: 52 pages)
- International standard alignment
- Regional leadership position
