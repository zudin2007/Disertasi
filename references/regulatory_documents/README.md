## Jurisdictions & Authorities

### 1. OJK (Otoritas Jasa Keuangan) - Indonesia

**Authority:** Indonesia's Financial Services Authority  
**Regulatory Scope:** Capital markets, banking, insurance, and digital financial services  
**Cryptocurrency Focus:** Crypto assets as commodity trading subject to OJK oversight  
**Key Framework:** Commodity Futures Trading Regulation (CFTD)

**Relevant Regulations:**
- CFTD Regulation on cryptocurrency trading
- Anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing (AML-CTF) requirements
- Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures for crypto exchanges
- Reporting requirements for digital asset service providers
- Islamic finance coordination guidelines

**Dissertation Integration:**
- APPENDIX_G: Phase 3 Case Study Protocol — Indonesia Implementation (G.1)
- CHAPTER_4: Regulatory Landscape — OJK jurisdiction section
- CHAPTER_6: Regulatory Harmonization — Full Integration Model

### 2. BNM (Bank Negara Malaysia) - Central Bank of Malaysia

**Authority:** Malaysia's Central Bank and Financial Regulator  
**Regulatory Scope:** Banking, payment systems, Islamic finance, and digital assets  
**Cryptocurrency Focus:** Digital asset framework with dual conventional/Islamic tracks  
**Key Framework:** Digital Assets Policy (DAP) and Shariah Governance Framework

**Relevant Regulations:**
- Digital Assets Operational Framework
- Shariah Governance Requirements for Digital Assets
- Islamic Banking and Takaful Act amendments for crypto
- Payment and Settlement Systems Act provisions
- Money Services Business licensing requirements
- Shariah Advisory Council guidance on cryptocurrency

### 3. VARA (Virtual Asset Regulatory Authority) - United Arab Emirates

**Authority:** UAE's Federal Virtual Asset Regulatory Authority  
**Regulatory Scope:** Cryptocurrency, tokens, digital assets, and crypto service providers

**Key Framework:** Virtual Asset Regulatory Framework (VARF) and Shariah Compliance Standards

**Relevant Regulations:**

## Regulatory Context Analysis

### Model Classifications

#### Indonesia (OJK) — Full Integration Model
**Characteristic:** Crypto regulation integrated into conventional commodity framework with Islamic finance coordination

**Key Documents Needed:**
1. Primary regulatory framework (CFTD Regulation)
2. Exchange operational standards
3. KYC and AML-CTF procedures
4. Shariah Board guidance
5. Consumer protection requirements
6. Reporting and compliance standards
7. Policy roadmap

**Framework Dimensions Impact:**
- D1 (Asset Backing): OJK requirements on asset reserving
- D2 (Value Stability): Commodity trading standards
- D3 (Productive Utility): Utility recognition in framework
- D4 (Governance): Exchange governance requirements
- D5 (Regulatory Recognition): Full OJK recognition scope

#### Malaysia (BNM) — Competitive Positioning Model
**Characteristic:** Dual conventional-Islamic regulatory tracks with advanced Shariah governance

**Key Documents Needed:**
1. Digital Assets Operational Framework
2. Islamic Banking Act amendments
3. Shariah Advisory Council guidance
4. Money Services Business requirements
5. Custody and safeguarding standards
6. Reporting and disclosure requirements
7. Islamic finance strategy

**Framework Dimensions Impact:**
- D1 (Asset Backing): Islamic backing standards
- D2 (Value Stability): Stablecoin requirements
- D3 (Productive Utility): Islamic productive financing
- D4 (Governance): Dual governance tracks (conventional + Islamic)
- D5 (Regulatory Recognition): BNM institutional recognition

#### UAE (VARA) — Regional Authority Model
**Characteristic:** Standalone virtual asset regulatory authority with regional coordination

**Key Documents Needed:**
1. Virtual Asset Service Provider Licensing Framework
2. Crypto Custodial Requirements
3. VARA Shariah Board standards
4. Market conduct standards
5. AML-CTF procedures
6. Islamic stablecoin recognition
7. Regional coordination protocols

**Framework Dimensions Impact:**
- D1 (Asset Backing): VARA custodial requirements
- D2 (Value Stability): Stablecoin framework
- D3 (Productive Utility): Utility token recognition
- D4 (Governance): VARA governance oversight
- D5 (Regulatory Recognition): VARA institutional recognition + regional coordination

## Document Acquisition Guide

### For Each Jurisdiction

1. **Visit Regulatory Authority Website:**
   - OJK: www.ojk.go.id (Cryptocurrency/Digital Assets section)
   - BNM: www.bnm.gov.my (Digital Assets/Islamic Finance sections)
   - VARA: www.vara.ae (Regulatory Framework section)

2. **Download or Request:**
   - Main regulatory frameworks (RF documents)
   - Shariah guidance (SC documents)
   - Operational requirements (OR documents)
   - Policy statements (PS documents)

3. **Organize by Category:**
   - Create subfolders: RF/, SC/, OR/, MC/, PS/
   - Use standardized file naming: [Category]-[Authority]-[Number]_[Title].pdf

4. **Update Metadata:**
   - Record file sizes and download dates
   - Add to REGULATORY_INDEX.md
   - Cross-reference to dissertation sections

5. **Commit Changes:**
   ```bash
   git add regulatory_documents/
   git commit -m "Add regulatory documents from [Authority]"
   ```
