## Model Characteristics: Competitive Positioning

**Dual-Track Approach:**
- Conventional track (RF-BNM-01)
- Islamic track (RF-BNM-02, RF-BNM-03, SC-BNM-02)

**Advanced Shariah Governance:**
- Shariah Advisory Council authority (SC-BNM-01)
- Institutional Shariah board requirements (RF-BNM-03)
- Islamic product development support (PS-BNM-02)

**Integration Points:**
- Islamic banking participation (RF-BNM-02)
- Dual licensing pathways (OR-BNM-01)
- Unified custody standards with Islamic options (OR-BNM-02)
