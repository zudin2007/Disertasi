## 🔍 Key Findings

### Islamic Legal Assessment

**Maqasid Shariah Scores:**
- **Pure Cryptocurrencies:** 2.4/5 (Fails necessity and certainty requirements)
- **Asset-Backed Alternatives:** 4.0-4.5/5 (Significantly higher compliance)
- **Stablecoins:** 4.0/5 (Addresses primary stability concerns)

**Key Insight:** Cryptocurrency Islamic compliance exists on continuum, not binary halal/haram determination.

### Jurisprudential Positions

**Implicit Consensus Among Islamic Authorities:**
1. Pure cryptocurrencies are non-compliant with Islamic law
2. Asset-backed digital assets merit serious consideration
3. Maqasid Shariah provides appropriate assessment framework

**Evolution:** Positions moved from 2017 categorical prohibition toward 2024 nuanced acceptance of qualified instruments.

### Regulatory Landscape

**Five Distinct Approaches Identified:**
- Indonesia: Consumer protection focus
- Malaysia: Innovation and technology support
- Saudi Arabia: Monetary control and stability
- UAE: Financial center competitive positioning
- Bahrain: Financial stability and risk management

**Key Finding:** Fragmentation reflects legitimate policy differentiation, not regulatory error.

### Classification Framework

**Five Categories Established:**
- **Category A (≥19 pts):** Islamic-Compliant → HALAL
- **Category B (15-19 pts):** Conditional-Compliance → MUBAH MA'A KARAHAH
- **Category C (10-14 pts):** Problematic → MAKRUH/HARAM
- **Category D (5-9 pts):** Speculative → HARAM
- **Category E (15-25 pts):** CBDC → HALAL

**Case Study Results:**
- Bitcoin: 13 points (Category C - Problematic)
- Ethereum: 12 points (Category C - Problematic)
- USDC: 19 points (Category B - Conditional, pathway to A)
- Islamic Digital Asset (Hypothetical): 21 points (Category A - Compliant)

### Regulatory Harmonization

**IIFRC Proposal:** Institutional framework enabling unified assessment while preserving jurisdictional sovereignty.

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Unified cryptocurrency assessment across jurisdictions
- Reduced regulatory arbitrage and compliance costs
- Clear pathways to Islamic compliance
- Progressive regulatory harmonization
